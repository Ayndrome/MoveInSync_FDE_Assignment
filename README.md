# MoveInSync App Review Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes 299 one-star reviews of the MoveInSync employee commute application from the Apple App Store and Google Play Store to identify major user pain points and recommend improvements.

## Key Findings
The five most common issue categories were:
- Location, ETA, and routing
- App reliability
- Booking and scheduling
- Login and access
- Feature usability

These categories accounted for approximately 74% of the categorized reviews.

## Recommendations
The report prioritizes improving trip tracking and location accuracy, investigating app reliability issues, and addressing booking and login problems.

## Methodology
1. Collected and cleaned app reviews.
2. Categorized reviews by their primary issue.
3. Compared issue frequencies to identify priorities.
4. Developed recommendations and a phased action plan.

## Limitations
This analysis uses a sample of one-star reviews and may not represent the experience of all MoveInSync users. The identified complaints indicate reported symptoms and do not independently establish their underlying technical causes.
